# Phase 2F — Remaining Gaps

366 of the 421 original cards are not fully simulated after Phase 2F: 358 partially simulated, 6 incorrectly simulated (known defects), and 2 that need an interaction test. They are listed card by card at the end, generated from the coverage data by `tools/completion.mjs`.

|---|---|---|---|

## Coverage checker gaps that remain

- A sentence the reader turns into something different (a misreading) is only found by the MISREAD checks and `tests/known-defects.json`. 2F found five more by hand (Will of the Jeskai, Inspiring Call, Knights of Round, Dalkovan Encampment, Incubation Druid). Others may exist among the "full" cards.
- The 2F-8 checker fix exposed restrictions on 38 more cards ("activate only if / during …"). Reading those restrictions is Phase 2G/2H work.
