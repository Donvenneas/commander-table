# Card coverage audit

Generated by `node tests/coverage.mjs --report` from tests/cards-snapshot.json: the 3500 most-played Commander cards as Scryfall listed them by EDHREC popularity when the snapshot was taken (the # column is that order). Each card is read with the game's own card reader, and every effect it produces is checked against the engine's handlers.

| Status | Cards | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| full | 2794 | every line read, every effect handled (328 of them named in a test; the other 2466 are read but not individually verified) |
| incorrect | 71 | read, but read as something else (a misreading found by the MISREAD checks in coverage.mjs) |
| partial | 406 | some lines or sentences do nothing |
| restriction | 86 | an unread restriction: the card plays stronger than printed |
| unhandled | 4 | the reader produces something no engine module handles |
| unsupported | 139 | none of its abilities is read |

"Full" means the text is read and every effect has a handler; it does not prove each handler is correct. Cards are checked one by one in tests/interactions.mjs and tests/abilities.mjs. "Incorrect" only holds the misreadings the MISREAD checks know about; others may exist among the full cards.
